To automatically attach invoices to credit control summaries, you must first select the invoice report to be used.

1.  Go to **Invoicing > Configuration > Settings**.
2.  Under the **Credit Control** section, locate the **Report Attachment** option.
3.  In the **Report to attach** field, choose the specific invoice report format you wish to send to customers (e.g., "Invoices").
4.  Click **Save** to apply the new settings.
